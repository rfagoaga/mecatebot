Status: PROVISIONAL. All figures are approximate and tentative. They describe the state of an

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0. WHAT THESE FIGURES ARE, AND WHAT THEY ARE NOT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The journal register is a work in progress. Journals enter it from about 80 bibliographic
  sources (directories, national registers, library catalogues, regional platforms). About
  250,000 further candidate records are still awaiting human review, so the number of
  journals will change.
- We have NOT harvested all articles or all citations of these journals. The article and
  citation figures below come only from what publishers deposited in Crossref, as recorded in
  the Crossref Public Data File (March 2026). Journals without DOIs, articles never registered
  in Crossref and references never deposited are not counted; for the Global South these are
  precisely the largest gaps.
- The Wikidata figures are a snapshot taken on 9 November 2026. Wikidata changes every day.
- Nothing here has been edited in Wikidata. All checks were read-only.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. JOURNAL REGISTER (build "e21", 8 November 2026)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Scope: social sciences and humanities, 87 disciplines (Digital Commons taxonomy, level 2),
late 19th century to 2025, with or without ISSN, active or ceased.
- Journals in the register (de-duplicated across disciplines): ~80,200
- Journals with a checksum-valid ISSN: ~70,300
- Journals with an anchored country (sources agree): ~62,900
- Journals present in Crossref (any DOI in the March 2026 file): ~46,300 (~58%)
- Journals that deposit at least some references in Crossref: ~20,000 (~25%)
Method: universe builder with provenance per observation; no automatic merges; keyword or
citation evidence alone never admits a journal (it only creates review candidates).

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. JOURNALS IN WIKIDATA (snapshot 9 November 2026)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Check 1 (bulk): SPARQL on P236 (ISSN) and P7363 (ISSN-L) for 111,587 ISSNs of the ~70,300
journals, in 732 queries; raw responses and receipts kept.
  Status of the journals with ISSN (approximate):
  - item exists and holds the journal's ISSNs ................ ~32,500
  - item exists but lacks an ISSN or the country (P495) ...... ~2,150
  - item's country differs from our anchored country ......... 13
  - two or more items for the same journal ................... ~950
  - one item holding ISSNs of several register records ....... ~640 records / 337 items
      (93 with the same title: usually print/online split in our register;
       244 with different titles: mostly title histories, possibly wrong merges)
  - not found ................................................ ~34,050 (~48%)

Check 2 (sample): 500 randomly chosen "not found" journals re-checked with the Wikidata
search API (haswbstatement on P236/P7363, plus a title search restricted to journal items):
  - found by ISSN (missed by SPARQL) ...... 1   (0.2%)
  - title-only candidates ................. 68  (13.6%; mostly generic titles such as
                                                  "Estudios", "Musique"; 23 = 4.6% with a
                                                  single candidate, for human review)
  - still absent .......................... 431 (86.2%)
Check 3 (all): the ~34,050 "not found" journals, every one re-checked with the search API
(10–11 November 2026; one query on all ISSNs, P236|P7363, and one title search among journal
items; raw responses kept):
  - found by ISSN (missed by SPARQL) ............ 39      (0.1%)
  - one title-only candidate (human review) ..... 1,830   (5.4%)
  - several generic-title candidates (review) ... 2,144   (6.3%)
  - no item ..................................... 30,041  (88.2%)
  Result: about 30,000 journals with ISSN (roughly 43% of the register's ISSN journals) have no
  Wikidata item. Title-only candidates are never treated as identity.

Absence by region after the full check (share of the register's ISSN journals with no item):
  Central Asia 71% · South-eastern Asia 69% · Africa 57% · Southern Asia 51% · Western Asia 48% ·
  Europe 36% · Latin America and the Caribbean 34% · Australia/New Zealand 30% · Northern America 30% ·
  Eastern Asia 28%.  Global South 56% vs Global North 34% (Global North = Northern America, Europe,
  Australia/New Zealand, Japan, South Korea, Israel, Taiwan, Singapore, Hong Kong).
  Countries (>=300 ISSN journals): Thailand 90%, Nigeria 77%, Indonesia 69%, Pakistan 61%, Malaysia 60%,
  India 53%; Brazil 31%, Mexico 33%; France 56%, Germany 50% (many ceased catalogue titles).
  Reference deposit in Crossref: Global South 17% of ISSN journals vs Global North 35%.
About half of the not-found journals (~17,600) do have DOIs in Crossref.

3. ARTICLES AND REFERENCES DEPOSITED IN CROSSREF (March 2026 public data file)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
These figures count only what is in Crossref for the register's journals with ISSN.
They are NOT a harvest of all articles or citations of these journals.
- Works of the register's journals in Crossref: ~26.2 million
- Works with at least one deposited reference: ~8.6 million (~33%)
- Deposited references: ~334.4 million, of which
    with a DOI ........................... ~187.8 million (~56%)
    with only a journal title ............ ~28.3 million
    with only unstructured text .......... ~68.2 million
    with other partial data only ......... ~50.0 million (e.g. author and year)
- Cited DOIs not found in Crossref: ~2.3 million
- Not yet measured: how many of these articles already exist in Wikidata (by DOI, P356).
  This will be measured and reported before any article edits.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. LIMITATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Register membership, discipline assignment and country anchoring are under review; figures
  can move by several percent.
- Crossref-based figures under-represent journals without DOIs and publishers that do not
  deposit references; they describe the visible part of the record, not the whole.
- SPARQL and search results can lag behind edits; every creation will be re-checked live at
  the moment of writing (see the bot request, "double verification").
- Title-only matches are never treated as identity.
